GLIDE-CHAT v21 — HOW TO DEPLOY (2 minutes)

1) Firebase Console -> Firestore Database -> Rules
   Paste the WHOLE text of RULES.txt -> Publish.
   (Make sure a Firestore Database exists: Standard edition, location us-central, then Create.)

2) Netlify (app.netlify.com/drop):
   Drag and drop this "gupshup" FOLDER (not the zip).
   To update an existing site: Site -> Deploys -> drop the folder in the "drag and drop" area at the bottom.

3) Firebase -> Authentication -> Settings -> Authorized domains
   Add your Netlify address (e.g. abc.netlify.app).

4) Open the site and press Ctrl+Shift+R.
   "Glide-Chat v21" must be written at the bottom-left. If it is not, the old version is still running.

To invite a friend: after login, tap "Send link" at the top of your room.
